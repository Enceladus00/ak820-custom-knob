What you can bind to the knob

You get turn, press and long press. A set of jobs for those is a mode, and a long press moves to the next one. Three come ready to use, and you can edit them or add your own.

Different knob functions in different apps

Pick which modes the knob has in each app, like a Timeline mode that only exists in your video editor. It switches by itself when another app comes to the front.

Click hints

Some websites take thirty presses of Tab to reach one button. Press Ctrl Alt H instead and everything clickable gets a letter. Type the letter to click it, or hold Shift to right-click.

It works in most apps and in Chrome and Edge, and you can change how the labels look.

Macros

Save key presses, typed text, waits and mouse clicks, then start them with a hotkey or a knob gesture. Press Record to capture keys with their timing and edit the steps afterwards. Esc stops a macro that's running.

Off until you want them

Mouse mode, hints and macros stay off until you turn them on in Settings. Until then only the knob changes, and every other key behaves as it always did.

Keyboard lighting layouts

Save layouts with an effect, colour, brightness and speed. There are seven to start from and 19 effects. The lights only change when you click Apply.

Have a different AK820 MAX?

This has only been tested on one AK820 MAX Ultra over its 2.4 GHz receiver. Other keyboards sold as AK820 MAX can use different hardware, and the USB cable and Bluetooth aren't tested yet. With your receiver plugged in, this PowerShell command lists it if it's the right one:

Get-PnpDevice -PresentOnly | Where-Object InstanceId -match 'VID_0C45&PID_FEFC'

You can also just install it. If the tray icon stays grey with a red badge while the receiver is plugged in, your version isn't supported yet, and the knob goes on controlling volume.

Questions

How do I remap the knob on the AJAZZ AK820 MAX Ultra?

Install AK820 MAX Ultra Toolbox, open Settings, and on the Modes page choose what turning, pressing and long-pressing the knob should do. Save, and the knob uses the new functions right away.

Can I bind a keyboard shortcut to the AK820 MAX Ultra knob?

Yes. Choose Press keys, click the key field and press any key or combination. Turning clockwise and counter-clockwise can each have their own.

Can the knob do different things in different apps?

Yes. The Apps page sets which modes the knob has in each app, and it switches automatically when another app comes to the front.

Can I use the AK820 MAX Ultra as a mouse?

Yes. Turn on Mouse mode and the arrow keys move the pointer, with the keys next to them for clicking and scrolling.

Can I record macros on the AK820 MAX Ultra?

Yes. On the Macros page press Record, press your keys, then give the macro a hotkey or bind it to the knob.

Do I need the official AJAZZ software?

No. AK820 MAX Ultra Toolbox works on its own.

Does it change my keyboard's firmware?

No. The knob functions all run on your PC and write nothing to the keyboard. Lighting settings are sent to the keyboard only when you click Apply, and they don't touch the firmware.

Does it work with the regular AK820 MAX, AK820 Pro, MAX Plus or MAX HE?

It has only been tested on an AK820 MAX Ultra over its 2.4 GHz receiver. Other models and versions use different hardware or haven't been tested. If yours isn't recognised, the program does nothing and the knob keeps working normally.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. It needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Is it official?

No. It is a free, open-source community project, not made or endorsed by AJAZZ, and provided as-is under the Apache 2.0 license.
