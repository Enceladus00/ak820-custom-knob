Remap the AJAZZ AK820 MAX Ultra knob on Windows

Bind the knob to volume, scrolling, zoom, media keys or any shortcut, with different functions in different apps. Add macros, a keyboard-controlled mouse and click hints too. Nothing changes until you turn it on.

Download for Windows View the code on GitHub

What you can bind to the knob

The knob has three gestures: turn, press and long press. Each mode gives them a job, and a long press switches to the next mode.

  • System volume, or the volume of only the app in front
  • Mute
  • Scrolling up, down or sideways, and zoom
  • Any key or key combination, with a different one per direction
  • Mouse clicks: left, middle or right
  • Different functions while you hold Ctrl, Shift, Alt or Win

These are the three built-in modes. You can change them or add your own.

Different knob functions in different apps

Choose which modes the knob has in each app, for example a Timeline mode in your video editor. It switches automatically when another app comes to the front.

Macros

Save a sequence of key presses, typed text, waits and mouse clicks, and start it with a hotkey or a knob gesture. Press Record to capture keys and the pauses between them, then edit the steps.

Use the mouse from your keyboard

Handy when you sit away from your desk. In mouse mode the arrow keys move the pointer, Right Ctrl and Right Shift click, and Page Up and Page Down scroll. All other keys keep typing as usual.

Click hints

No more pressing Tab over and over to reach a button. Press a key and a small letter label appears on everything clickable in the app or website in front. Type a label to click it.

Keyboard lighting layouts

Save lighting layouts with an effect, a colour or colour cycling, brightness and speed, then apply one with a click. It comes with seven layouts and 19 effects.

Have a different AK820 MAX?

This program has only been tested on the AK820 MAX Ultra. Other versions sold as AK820 MAX can use different hardware, and it only recognises a 2.4 GHz receiver that Windows sees as VID_0C45&PID_FEFC. With the receiver plugged in, run this in PowerShell:

Get-PnpDevice -PresentOnly | Where-Object InstanceId -match 'VID_0C45&PID_FEFC'

If it lists devices, you have that receiver. You can also just install it: if the tray icon stays grey with a red badge while the receiver is plugged in, your version isn't supported yet. Nothing changes for your keyboard in that case. The USB cable and Bluetooth aren't tested yet.

Questions

How do I remap the knob on the AJAZZ AK820 MAX Ultra?

Install AK820 MAX Ultra Toolbox, open Settings, and on the Modes page choose what turning, pressing and long-pressing the knob should do. Save, and the knob uses the new functions right away.

Can I bind a keyboard shortcut to the AK820 MAX Ultra knob?

Yes. Choose Press keys, click the key field and press any key or combination. Turning clockwise and counter-clockwise can each have their own.

Can the knob do different things in different apps?

Yes. The Apps page sets which modes the knob has in each app, and it switches automatically when another app comes to the front.

Can I use the AK820 MAX Ultra as a mouse?

Yes. Turn on Mouse mode and the arrow keys move the pointer, with the keys next to them for clicking and scrolling.

Can I record macros on the AK820 MAX Ultra?

Yes. On the Macros page press Record, press your keys, then give the macro a hotkey or bind it to the knob.

Do I need the official AJAZZ software?

No. AK820 MAX Ultra Toolbox works on its own.

Does it change my keyboard's firmware?

No. The knob functions all run on your PC and write nothing to the keyboard. Lighting settings are sent to the keyboard only when you click Apply, and they don't touch the firmware.

Does it work with the regular AK820 MAX, AK820 Pro, MAX Plus or MAX HE?

It has only been tested on an AK820 MAX Ultra over its 2.4 GHz receiver. Other models and versions use different hardware or haven't been tested. If yours isn't recognised, the program does nothing and the knob keeps working normally.

Does it work on Mac or Linux?

Not at the moment. It needs Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
